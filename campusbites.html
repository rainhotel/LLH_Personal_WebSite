01 / THE VENTURE THESIS

不是再做一个点评页。
而是解决校园里的信息断层。

Not another review page.
A response to fragmented campus information.

产品从一个高频、具体的问题出发：学生每天都要做吃饭决策，但店铺、菜品、价格、位置和真实体验散落在不同渠道。商户缺少持续反馈，校园运营也缺少结构化信息。CampusBites 尝试把这些信息放回同一个可治理的产品体系。

The product starts with a frequent, concrete decision. Students choose where and what to eat every day, yet shops, dishes, prices, locations, and real experiences are scattered. Merchants lack structured feedback, while campus operators lack a consistent information layer. CampusBites brings these pieces into one governed product system.

02 / PRODUCT SYSTEM

一个产品，
四个相互咬合的工作面。

One product.
Four connected operating surfaces.

我把校园本地生活拆成学生体验、商户工具、平台运营和 AI 服务，而不是把所有能力塞进一个小程序页面。不同角色有自己的入口，但共享同一套业务事实和治理规则。

CampusBites is organized into the student experience, merchant tools, platform operations, and AI services—not squeezed into a single mini-program surface. Each role has its own workflow while sharing the same business facts and governance rules.

03 / BUSINESS MODEL

技术不是终点。
产品必须找到可持续的价值交换。

Technology is not the destination.
The product needs a sustainable exchange of value.

商业化不是先卖广告，而是先让学生愿意使用、商户愿意维护信息、运营方愿意依赖数据。只有三方价值成立，收费才有基础。

Monetization should not start with selling ad slots. Students must find value, merchants must want to maintain their information, and operators must be able to act on the data. Monetization follows only when that exchange works.

01 / ARCHITECTURE

先做模块化单体

以 Spring Boot 组织领域模块，复用 MySQL、Redis 和既有部署能力；不为低数据量阶段引入不必要的微服务、Kafka 或向量数据库。

Organize domain modules inside Spring Boot and reuse MySQL, Redis, and existing deployment infrastructure. Avoid microservices, Kafka, or vector databases before the workload justifies them.

02 / DATA OWNERSHIP

事实必须有归属

MySQL 保存可恢复的业务事实；Redis 负责缓存、短期队列和协调。模型输出不能覆盖学校范围、店铺身份、公开状态、价格等服务端事实。

MySQL owns recoverable business facts; Redis handles cache, short-lived queues, and coordination. Model output cannot override school scope, shop identity, visibility, or prices.

03 / AI BOUNDARIES

模型只做受控决策

Meal Agent 采用模型理解、工具检索、候选选择和最终校验的图式流程；硬预算、地点、店型和证据由服务端验证。

The Meal Agent follows a graph workflow of model interpretation, tool retrieval, candidate selection, and final validation. Budget, location, shop type, and evidence are checked server-side.

04 / RELIABILITY

机器人不是一个 webhook

QQBot 包含 Gateway 生命周期、单活租约、事件去重、有界执行队列、心跳与重连、交互回执、失败分类和 Trace。

QQBot includes gateway lifecycle management, a single-active lease, event deduplication, bounded executors, heartbeats and reconnects, interaction acknowledgements, failure mapping, and traces.

05 / OBSERVABILITY

让运营能看见系统

运营后台把模型配置、运行状态、群组策略、检索证据、重排结果和失败原因放进可追踪的工作流，而不是只显示一个“AI 已开启”。

The operations console connects model configuration, runtime state, group policies, retrieval evidence, reranking, and failure reasons into an observable workflow—not just an “AI enabled” switch.

06 / EVALUATION

先建立评测，再谈效果

Agent benchmark 使用可重复生成的合成数据和逐字段 oracle，覆盖菜品、预算、地点、店名、多轮上下文与安全边界。测试覆盖不等于线上准确率。

The Agent benchmark uses reproducible synthetic data and field-level oracles for dishes, budgets, locations, shop names, multi-turn context, and safety boundaries. Test coverage is not online accuracy.
